## Introduction

Grupo HL, made up of HL Ingenieros S.A., HL Infraestructura S.A.S. and ECOHL S.A.S., as data controller, recognizes the importance of protecting the information of every natural person it deals with, including employees, former employees, suppliers, partners, clients, visitors and other stakeholders.

This policy sets out the general guidelines for the collection, use, storage, circulation and deletion of personal data, in compliance with Law 1581 of 2012, Decree 1377 of 2013 and other related regulations.

Processing will be based on the principles of legality, purpose, freedom, accuracy, security and confidentiality, guaranteeing the rights of data subjects at all times.

## 1. General provisions

### 1.1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to define the rules governing the processing of personal data at Grupo HL, ensuring that data subjects know how their information is collected, used and protected, and that their rights are respected under Colombian data protection law.

### 1.2. Legal framework

This policy is based on the Colombian legal framework in force for personal data protection, in particular Statutory Law 1581 of 2012, Decree 1377 of 2013, Decree 1074 of 2015 and any rules that supplement, amend or regulate them. It also adheres to the constitutional principles set out in articles 15 and 20 of the Political Constitution of Colombia, and to the guidelines of the Superintendency of Industry and Commerce as the supervisory authority.

### 1.3. Definitions

**Authorization:** Prior, express and informed consent of the data subject for the processing of their data.

**Privacy notice:** Document addressed to the data subject informing them of the processing policy and its purposes.

**Database:** Organized set of personal data subject to processing.

**Personal data:** Any information that identifies or can identify a natural person.

**Sensitive data:** Information that affects the data subject's privacy or whose misuse may lead to discrimination, such as health data, political or religious orientation, or ethnic origin, among others.

**Private data:** Confidential information relevant only to the data subject, such as medical history or personal habits.

**Semi-private data:** Information that is neither intimate nor public, which may be of interest to certain persons or sectors, such as credit history.

**Public data:** Information not subject to confidentiality, available by legal mandate or because it is in public records.

**Biometric data:** Data relating to unique physical characteristics such as fingerprints, facial recognition or iris.

**Archive document:** Physical or digital record containing information collected in the course of institutional activities.

**Data processor:** Natural or legal person who processes data on behalf of the controller.

**Information source:** Entity that supplies data to the operator or user.

**Classified public information:** Information that, for legitimate reasons, cannot be freely disclosed.

**Restricted information:** Data exempt from public access to protect sensitive private or public interests.

**Information operator:** Whoever manages and delivers personal data under the conditions set by law.

**Data subject:** Natural person whose personal data is processed.

**Data controller:** Natural or legal person who decides on the database and/or the processing.

**User:** Person who accesses personal data, with prior authorization, as a recipient.

**Processing:** Any operation on personal data such as collection, storage, use, circulation or deletion.

### 1.4. Guiding principles of processing

Grupo HL guarantees that personal data will be processed in accordance with the following principles established by Law 1581 of 2012:

- **Legality:** Processing will follow the regulations in force.
- **Purpose:** Data will be processed for legitimate, specific purposes disclosed to the data subject.
- **Freedom:** Data will only be processed with the data subject's prior authorization, except for legal exceptions.
- **Accuracy:** Information will be truthful, complete, up to date and understandable.
- **Transparency:** Data subjects may learn at any time how their data is used.
- **Restricted access and circulation:** Only legally authorized persons will access the data.
- **Security:** Measures will be applied to protect data against unauthorized access or fraud.
- **Confidentiality:** Non-public data will be treated confidentially, even after the contractual relationship ends.

### 1.5. Rights of data subjects

Under Law 1581 of 2012, Grupo HL guarantees data subjects the following rights:

- a. To know, access, update and correct their personal data.
- b. To request proof of the authorization granted for its processing.
- c. To be informed of how their information has been used.
- d. To revoke the authorization and/or request deletion of their data when legal principles are not respected.
- e. To file complaints with the Superintendency of Industry and Commerce.
- f. To access their data free of charge at least once per calendar month.
- g. To refrain from answering questions about sensitive data or about information on children and adolescents, except where legally permitted.

### 1.6. Duties of the data controller and the data processor

Under Law 1581 of 2012 and Decree 1377 of 2013, Grupo HL, as data controller, and any third parties acting as processors must fulfill the following duties:

#### 1.6.1. Duties of the data controller

- a. Guarantee the data subject the full exercise of their rights.
- b. Request and keep a copy of the data subject's authorization.
- c. Inform the data subject of the purpose of the processing.
- d. Keep the data under secure conditions.
- e. Update, correct and delete data when appropriate.
- f. Handle inquiries and claims within the legal deadlines.
- g. Inform the processor of any change related to the data.
- h. Require the processor to comply with security and confidentiality rules.
- i. Adopt an internal manual of policies and procedures.
- j. Report security incidents to the Superintendency of Industry and Commerce.

#### 1.6.2. Duties of the data processor

- a. Guarantee the data subject's rights at all times.
- b. Keep the information under secure conditions.
- c. Update, correct or delete data as instructed by the controller.
- d. Record notes such as "claim in process" or "information under legal dispute" when applicable.
- e. Refrain from circulating information that has been disputed or blocked.
- f. Allow access only to authorized persons.
- g. Follow the instructions of the controller and of the Superintendency of Industry and Commerce.

When the same person acts as both controller and processor, they must fulfill the duties of both roles.

### 2.5. Authorization

Grupo HL will process personal data with the data subject's prior authorization, given freely, in advance, expressly and on an informed basis, except in the cases where the law establishes exceptions.

Authorization may be obtained by physical, electronic or verbal means, or through unequivocal conduct from which it can be concluded that the data subject consents to the processing. In all cases, Grupo HL will keep proof of the authorization.

The authorization forms used will depend on the type of relationship with the stakeholder, such as employees, suppliers, visitors or candidates.

#### 2.5.1. Authorization to use images (photography and video)

In the course of its activities, Grupo HL may capture images of natural persons (clients, suppliers, subcontractors, visitors) through photographs or videos.

- For third parties, prior express authorization will be requested.
- For employees, the authorization is provided for in the employment contract.

These images may be used for informational, institutional or documentary purposes, always in compliance with this policy.

#### 2.5.2. Cases in which authorization is not required

Under Law 1581 of 2012, the data subject's authorization is not required in the following cases:

- a. Information required by a public or judicial entity in the exercise of its functions.
- b. Data of a public nature.
- c. Medical or health emergencies.
- d. Processing authorized by law for historical, statistical or scientific purposes.
- e. Data related to the civil registry of persons.

In these cases, processing will follow the principles and guarantees established by law.

### 2.9. Risk assessment

Grupo HL incorporates into its Integrated Management System (IMS) a preventive approach to identify, assess and mitigate the risks associated with the processing of personal data.

As part of this process, a Personal Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) is carried out periodically, including:

- Identification of internal and external threats.
- Analysis of vulnerabilities and security gaps.
- Assessment of impact and likelihood of occurrence.
- Definition of technical, administrative and legal controls to mitigate risks.

The documentation resulting from these assessments is available to supervisory authorities or entities that require it, as provided by law.

This approach allows Grupo HL to ensure information security, business continuity and respect for data subjects' rights.
